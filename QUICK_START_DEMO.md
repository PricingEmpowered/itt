## The 5-Minute Power Demo

### Slide 1: Dashboard (30 seconds)
**Click**: Dashboard

**Say**:
> "This is PriceSpace - a complete CPQ platform managing 3,800+ quotes across 1,000+ products and 400 customers. Notice the real-time deal score analytics showing our quote quality is consistently above 85."

**Point out**: Quote activity, deal score trends, commission tracking

---

### Slide 2: Create Quote (90 seconds)
**Click**: Create Quote

**Say**:
> "Let me show you how fast we can create a quote. Watch the automation."

**Do**:
1. Select "Tech Innovations Inc" (Enterprise customer)
2. Select "Enterprise Customers" price list
3. Search "Actuator" → Add 50 units
   - **Point out**: "Notice the quantity break discount automatically applied"
4. Search "Bearing" → Add 100 units
   - **Point out**: "15% volume discount kicked in"
5. Click Services → Add "Premium Support"
   - **Point out**: "Adding recurring revenue"

**Say**:
> "See the deal score? It's 87 - that's excellent. This predicts high win probability and qualifies the rep for a commission bonus. The system is guiding us to better pricing decisions in real-time."

6. Click "Save Quote"

**Say**:
> "Done. Quote generated, commission calculated, approval workflow triggered if needed. What took 45 minutes in spreadsheets now takes 2 minutes."

---

### Slide 3: Deal Score Intelligence (60 seconds)
**Click**: Deal Score Analytics

**Say**:
> "Here's the secret sauce - our deal scoring engine analyzes every quote across 5 dimensions: margin health, competitive positioning, deal velocity, customer fit, and discount discipline."

**Point out**:
- Average score of 85-90
- Distribution chart
- Win rate correlation

**Say**:
> "Quotes scoring above 70 win 40% more often and have 25% better margins. This is predictive intelligence built into every deal. Sales reps get instant feedback, managers get visibility, and bad deals get caught before they happen."

---

### Slide 4: Commission Automation (60 seconds)
**Click**: Commissions

**Say**:
> "Commissions are automatically calculated when quotes are created. But here's what makes it powerful - we tie commission rates to BOTH deal size AND deal quality."

**Point to a commission record**:
> "See this $200,000 deal with a score of 85? The rep earns 6% base plus 2% bonus = 8% total, that's $16,000. Same deal with a score of 65? Only 6%, no bonus. That's $12,000."

**Say**:
> "This creates alignment. Reps are incentivized to maintain good margins and avoid heavy discounting. We've got 30 deals here worth $257,000 in commissions - all tracked automatically."

---

### Slide 5: Price Intelligence (60 seconds)
**Click**: Price Alerts

**Say**:
> "Cost management is proactive, not reactive. We're tracking 8 expected cost increases from suppliers. Each alert shows the impact and recommends price adjustments."

**Click**: Select 2-3 alerts → "Update Selected"

**Say**:
> "You can choose your strategy: maintain current margin percentage, maintain dollar profit, or use our recommended target. Then update hundreds of prices with one click."

**Say**:
> "This protects profitability automatically. No more surprises, no more margin squeeze."

---

> - **Automated**: Commissions, approvals, discounts - all automatic
> - **Protected**: Proactive cost management and margin controls
>

**If demo environment has issues**:
1. Have screenshots ready in presentation
2. Walk through user journey conceptually
3. Focus on value propositions and business case
4. Offer to send recorded demo afterward
5. Schedule technical follow-up

**If prospect seems disengaged**:
1. Stop and ask: "What's most important to you?"
2. Jump to that specific feature
3. Skip the script, have a conversation
4. Find their pain point and address it directly
